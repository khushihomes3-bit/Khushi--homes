KHUSHI HOMES Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

KHUSHI HOMES is a smart-home application that helps users manage supported home devices and related account features. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use KHUSHI HOMES.

1. Information we process

Depending on the features you use, KHUSHI HOMES may process your email address and account authentication information, smart-home device names and configuration details, camera connection details that you choose to enter, subscription and payment-related information, and support or enquiry information that you submit.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide account access, operate smart-home features, save your device settings, process subscriptions and payments, provide support, maintain security, and improve the service.

3. Payments

Payments are handled through the payment provider presented during checkout. KHUSHI HOMES does not ask you to send card, UPI, or banking credentials directly to us through chat or support.

4. Camera and device information

If you add a camera or smart-home device, you control the connection details you enter. Do not enter credentials or connection information that you are not authorized to use. Camera URLs and device settings may be stored so the features you request can work.

5. Sharing and service providers

We may use trusted service providers for authentication, application hosting, database services, payments, and other infrastructure needed to provide KHUSHI HOMES. Information is shared only as needed to provide those services, comply with law, protect users, or operate the service.

6. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Account deletion

You can request deletion of your KHUSHI HOMES account and associated application data through the in-app Delete Account feature or the public account-deletion page.
